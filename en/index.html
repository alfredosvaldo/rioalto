Services

One contractor from the first breaker hit to the final certificate. Our own machinery and a team with more than 20 years on mining sites.

Large-scale and interior demolition, and mechanised dismantling of plants, tanks and site facilities.

  • Mechanised dismantling
  • Large-scale demolition
  • Diamond wire sawing and cutting
  • Dismantling of site facilities
  • Perimeter fencing
  • Permit management

We sort, transport and manage the final disposal of C&D waste, ferrous material and hazardous waste, with full traceability.

  • Final disposal of ferrous and non-ferrous material
  • Transport of hazardous and non-hazardous waste
  • Inerting of contaminated hazardous waste
  • Mechanised on-site sorting

Excavation and enabling works to clear the site and leave it ready for the next construction stage.

  • Bulk excavation
  • Environmental remediation of industrial assets
  • Underpinning piles
  • Ground anchors
  • C&D waste management and final disposal

We buy and recover ferrous material across Chile and return it to the recycling chain, with every load certified.

  • Purchase of ferrous scrap in all its forms
  • Inerting of contaminated ferrous scrap (fuel tanks, generators, machinery)
  • On-site collection in 8 to 20 m³ containers
  • Collection by SEREMI-authorised trucks
  • Final disposal certificate

The route of every load, documented.

We sort material on site and haul it in trucks authorised by the regional health authority (SEREMI de Salud) to an authorised final destination. You receive the certificate.

  1. 1

    Survey and permits

    Inspection, work plan and permits before any machine goes in.

  2. 2

    Controlled demolition

    Our own equipment and mining-site safety standards.

  3. 3

    On-site sorting

    Ferrous, non-ferrous, C&D waste and hazardous waste, each on its own route.

  4. 4

    Authorised transport

    SEREMI-certified trucks and 8 to 20 m³ containers on site.

  5. 5

    Disposal and certificate

    Authorised final destination and a certificate for your file.
